<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"> </p> <h3>Weeks 10-12: Machine Building</h3> <h4>Pipe Robot Project</h4> <h5>Overview</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> During Machine Building Week, our class undertook an ambitious project to develop a solution for unclogging a sewage system in the Science Center. This collaborative effort involved the entire class working together to design and build a pipe robot capable of navigating and clearing obstructions in complex pipe systems. </p> <h5>Initial Research and Brainstorming</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> We began the project with a comprehensive brainstorming session, exploring various ideas and researching existing sewage unclogging systems. This phase was crucial in understanding the challenges we faced and the potential solutions available. </p> <div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center; margin: 20px 0;"> <img src="[Insert Image Path]" alt="Brainstorming session" style="width: 50vw;"> </div> <h5>Team Structure and Responsibilities</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> Following our initial research, we divided into specialized subgroups, each focusing on a critical aspect of the robot's functionality: </p> <ul style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> <li>Robot Steering</li> <li>Locomotion</li> <li>Camera Systems</li> <li>Signaling and Communication</li> </ul> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> I was assigned to the "Snakehead" team, responsible for developing the robot's steering mechanism. </p> <h5>Snakehead Team: Steering Development</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> Our team's primary focus was on creating an effective steering system for the robot. We began by laser cutting various parts and initially used strong wires to connect around the robot's head. As the project progressed, we went through several iterations, constantly refining our design. A significant part of our work involved close collaboration with the locomotion team to ensure the robot could move effectively. </p> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> Over time, we transitioned from an independent steering mechanism to integrating steering capabilities into the entire body of the robot. One of our main challenges was designing a system that could navigate the tight turns within the pipes while maintaining straight movement in linear sections. </p> <h5>Joystick and Screen Casing</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> In addition to my work on the steering mechanism, I personally took on the task of fabricating the casing for the joysticks and screen. This involved 3D modeling and printing custom parts to house these critical control components. </p> <div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center; margin: 20px 0;"> <img src="[Insert Image Path]" alt="Joystick 3D model and printed parts" style="width: 50vw;"> </div> <h5>Learnings and Challenges of Large Team Collaboration</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> Working on this large-scale project with multiple teams provided valuable insights into collaborative engineering: </p> <ul style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> <li><strong>Communication:</strong> The importance of clear and constant communication, especially between different subgroups, became evident throughout the project.</li> <li><strong>Time Management:</strong> Operating under a tight two-week schedule required efficient planning and execution.</li> <li><strong>Unified Vision:</strong> Developing and maintaining an overall vision for the project was crucial in aligning the efforts of all team members.</li> </ul> </div> </div>
