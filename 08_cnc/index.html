<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"> </p> <h3>Week 8: CNC Milling</h3> <h4>CNC Week Assignment</h4> <h5>Function of CNC Milling Machines</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> Computer Numerical Control (CNC) milling machines operate by following a set of programmed instructions to guide cutting tools along multiple axes. These machines can perform various operations such as cutting, drilling, and shaping materials like wood, plastic, and metal (using endmills, not drill bits). The process begins with a 3D CAD model, which is then converted into a DXF file that directs the machine's movements. </p> <h5>Subtractive Manufacturing and Its Variants</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> CNC milling differs from other subtractive methods in its ability to create complex 3D shapes. Unlike laser cutting, which is primarily used for 2D cutting and engraving, CNC milling allows for intricate 3D designs and precision. </p> <h4>Project Implementation</h4> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> To begin my CNC project, I created an illustration using Adobe Illustrator instead of Fusion 360. This design was then exported as a DXF file, a format compatible with the CNC milling machine. </p> <div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center; margin: 20px 0;"> <img src="[Insert Image Path]" alt="Adobe Illustrator design and exported DXF file" style="width: 50vw;"> </div> <h5>Machine Calibration</h5> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> Before milling, I performed the following calibration steps: </p> <ol style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> <li>Secured plywood material to the work surface using a nail gun</li> <li>Carefully calibrated the X, Y, and Z axes of the physical mill to ensure precise cutting</li> </ol> <div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; align-items: center; margin: 20px 0;"> <img src="[Insert Image Path]" alt="CNC machine calibration process" style="width: 50vw;"> </div> <p style="font: 15px Montserrat-Light;"> The milling process involved the machine following the programmed instructions to cut the design into the plywood.
